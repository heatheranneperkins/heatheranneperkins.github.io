vuedesk

Making workplace booking feel simple, while giving organisations the tools to manage spaces, policies and utilisation.

RoleProduct definition, UX/UI and interaction design
PlatformResponsive web SaaS
Focus0→1 product, self-service UX, booking journeys

Interactive floorplans help people find and book available spaces. Screenshots use demonstration data.

The opportunity

Booking a desk shouldn't be difficult.

Research into existing workplace booking tools revealed complicated pricing tiers and setup experiences. We saw an opportunity for a simpler, self-service alternative that combined desk, meeting-room and parking booking with straightforward administration and reporting.

I helped shape the product proposition and led product definition and design, working closely with a small engineering team to bring the initial MVP to life rapidly.

Make the everyday task effortless

For employees, the main task is simple: find an appropriate space and reserve it. The interface brings availability into familiar calendar and floorplan views, keeping the act of booking close to the context in which the decision is made.

Meeting-room availability in a calendar-style view.

Booking a specific desk directly from the floorplan.

Keep complexity where it belongs

The needs of an administrator are very different from those of an occasional booker. Administrators need to manage spaces, people, access and workplace policies; everyday users should not have to think about any of that unless it affects their booking.

I separated those experiences, keeping routine booking focused while allowing more detailed configuration through dedicated administration screens.

Organisation settings and permissions.

More granular administrative control for growing teams.

Turn usage into useful information

The product also gives workplace managers a view of how spaces are being used. I designed reporting screens that surface key measures at a glance and provide visual breakdowns for further investigation.

Utilisation reporting for workplace administrators.

Working from idea to product

We shipped an initial MVP in approximately two weeks, followed by additional functionality for hybrid policies, parking, integrations and reporting. Early feedback helped identify more detailed enterprise administration requirements.

Design principle

Simple for the many, capable for the few. The best everyday experience often depends on carefully containing complexity in the places where it is actually needed.

This project reflects the part of design I particularly enjoy: combining user experience, commercial thinking and close collaboration with engineering to turn an idea into working software.
